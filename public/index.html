Standing rules

  • Johor's weekend is Saturday and Sunday. Johor moved its rest days back to Saturday and Sunday on 1 January 2025, so both sides of the Causeway now share the same weekend. CNA, 7 Oct 2024
  • Malaysia enforces its own VEP on Singapore cars. Singapore-registered vehicles need a Malaysian VEP tag to enter Johor. Enforcement began in phases from 1 October 2024. The Star, 27 Sep 2024
  • Leave Singapore with three-quarters of a tank. Singapore-registered cars leaving through Woodlands or Tuas must carry at least three-quarters of a tank of fuel. Singapore Customs enforces it, with fines of up to S$500. Singapore Customs
  • Live traffic by phone. ICA's CHECK-TIPS hotline for the checkpoint traffic situation is 6863 0117. ICA website

How the forecast works

Each day in the window gets a score for each direction. Friday evening and Saturday morning add two points towards Johor, Sunday evening adds two points back to Singapore. The eve of a public holiday on either side adds two points towards Johor, and the last day of a break adds two points back. The day before a school holiday starts, and the day it ends, add two points in the matching direction, and every day inside a school holiday adds one point both ways. Three points is High, two is Medium.

The calendars behind it: Singapore public holidays and Malaysian national plus Johor holidays from the public Google holiday calendars, topped up with the holidays and replacement days those calendars miss, MOE school terms for 2026 and 2027, and the KPM Group B calendars for 2026 and 2027 that Johor follows, festive school closures included. The ICA record figures come from its media releases. All of it is in one dated file; if a date is wrong, fix it there and the next issue is right.
