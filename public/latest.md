## Rules that bite

- **Already in force: No SG Arrival Card without a valid Singapore VEP.** Since 6 October 2026, nobody travelling in a foreign-registered car can submit the SG Arrival Card unless the car already holds a valid Singapore Vehicle Entry Permit from LTA. That includes every passenger. The permit needs Singapore motor insurance, so sort both before the trip. [ICA Deepavali traffic advisory, Nov 2026](https://www.ica.gov.sg/about-ica/news-and-publications/media-room/heavy-traffic-expected-at-land-checkpoints-during-upcoming-deepavali-long-weekend)
- **Already in force: Motorcycles can use automated car lanes leaving Woodlands.** Since 15 October 2026, motorcyclists and pillion riders leaving Singapore through Woodlands can clear immigration by face recognition at selected departure car lanes. [The Straits Times, 9 Oct 2026](https://www.straitstimes.com/singapore/selected-woodlands-checkpoint-departure-car-lanes-open-to-motorcyclists-pillion-riders-from-oct-15)
- **Already in force: Unpaid Singapore fines block the VEP and entry.** From 2 November 2026, a foreign-registered car, motorcycle, taxi, bus or goods vehicle with unpaid Singapore traffic, parking or emissions fines cannot apply for or renew its VEP, and ICA will refuse it entry at Woodlands and Tuas. [LTA and MHA joint release, 15 May 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/5/news-releases/denying-vep-for-foreign-registered-vehicles-with-outstanding-fines.html)
- **From Monday 16 November 2026: More cross-border taxis, two more booking apps.** From 16 November 2026, the cross-border taxi scheme grows from 300 to 500 licensed taxis per country, and CDG Zig and Kumpool join Grab as licensed booking platforms. [CNA, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/cross-border-taxi-quota-increase-singapore-malaysia-6529936)
- **From Sunday 20 December 2026: Jalan Jim Quee beside the JB checkpoint closes to private cars.** Johor's transport councillor says the road beside the Johor Bahru checkpoint will take only buses and taxis from 20 December 2026, with e-hailing pick-ups moved to Jalan Tun Razak. No official notice yet, and the same paper reported 2027 in September, so check before relying on it. [The Straits Times, 8 Oct 2026](https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/se-asia/how-a-one-month-buffer-and-jb-transport-upgrades-will-prepare-commuters-for-the-rts-link)
- **From Thursday 31 December 2026: Last day for the cheaper ERP2 unit for foreign cars.** Foreign-registered vehicles can buy Singapore's ERP2 on-board unit at S$158.70 until 31 December 2026. The unit is optional, but without one a vehicle pays a flat road-pricing fee from 2027. [LTA, 6 Feb 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/2/news-releases/updates-foreign-registered-vehicles-entering-singapore.html)
- **From Friday 1 January 2027: Singapore VEP rises to S$50 a day, free days end.** From 1 January 2027, the VEP for foreign cars rises from S$35 to S$50 a day and for motorcycles from S$4 to S$7. It still does not apply on weekends and Singapore public holidays, but the 10 free days a year and the free weekday hours end. A vehicle without an ERP2 unit pays a flat S$10 (motorcycles S$3) on each day road pricing runs. The goods vehicle permit rises from S$40 to S$70 a month. [LTA, 6 Feb 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/2/news-releases/updates-foreign-registered-vehicles-entering-singapore.html)
- **From Friday 1 January 2027: Malaysian taxis pay S$15 a trip into Singapore.** From 1 January 2027, the permit for a Malaysia-registered taxi entering Singapore changes from S$2 a month to S$15 a trip. Expect cross-border fares to rise. [LTA, 25 Jun 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/6/news-releases/update-for-malaysia-registered-taxis-entering-singapore.html)
- **Expected February 2027, no exact date yet: RTS Link opening targeted for February 2027.** The operator has told both governments that the Johor Bahru to Woodlands North rail link should open in February 2027, later than the earlier end-2026 target, subject to independent safety certification and final approval. No exact date yet, and fares are due by the end of 2026. Five minutes station to station, up to 10,000 passengers an hour each way, immigration for both countries cleared at the departure station. [LTA and Malaysia MOT joint release, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/10/news-releases/grantors-informed-rts-link-service-targeted-feb-2027.html)

Standing rules:

- **Johor's weekend is Saturday and Sunday.** Johor moved its rest days back to Saturday and Sunday on 1 January 2025, so both sides of the Causeway now share the same weekend. [CNA, 7 Oct 2024](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/asia/malaysia-johor-weekend-days-change-saturday-sunday-january-2025-4662706)
- **Malaysia enforces its own VEP on Singapore cars.** Singapore-registered vehicles need a Malaysian VEP tag to enter Johor. Enforcement began in phases from 1 October 2024. [The Star, 27 Sep 2024](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2024/09/27/vep-enforcement-for-singapore-vehicles-to-start-in-phases-from-oct-1-says-jpj)
- **Leave Singapore with three-quarters of a tank.** Singapore-registered cars leaving through Woodlands or Tuas must carry at least three-quarters of a tank of fuel. Singapore Customs enforces it, with fines of up to S$500. [Singapore Customs](https://www.customs.gov.sg/at-customs/departing-singapore/three-quarter-tank-rule/)
- **Live traffic by phone.** ICA's CHECK-TIPS hotline for the checkpoint traffic situation is 6863 0117. [ICA website](https://www.ica.gov.sg/)
